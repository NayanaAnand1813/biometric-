ℹ️ How Training Works

The LBPH (Local Binary Patterns Histograms) algorithm reads all 200 × 200 grayscale faces from the faces/ directory. It analyzes local micro-texture patterns (edges, spots, flat areas) around each pixel and constructs a spatial histogram representing each person's facial geometry.

The trained weights are persisted to models/face_model.yml, and integer labels are written to models/labels.json. The model remains resident in memory for zero-latency live recognition.
